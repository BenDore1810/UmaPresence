# Consolidated live acceptance

Use the hashes in release-manifest.json and acceptance.json. Record PASS, FAIL or UNTESTED with scene, observation and evidence; never transfer a pass from another binary or game build.

## Session A — Standalone

Begin with a separate legitimate clean JP DMM installation when available. From a fresh ZIP extraction, verify discovery/Browse, explicit helper download/offline verification, game-closed install and clear completion message. Unknown-loader and malformed-config checks must preserve files. Do not change a career or spend game resources.

Launch once. Verify actually loaded Standalone and Hachimi absent. Visit Home, existing career record, normal Legacy, bare Legacy, an existing replay and skipped results. Compare official margins and winner wording. Watch at least two complete three-entry rotation cycles, including duplicate identities in different slots where available. Inspect English and Japanese tooltips and artwork on desktop and phone.

Enable duplicate casting in Concert settings, save, and select the same Uma in two slots. Confirm that the concert starts with both slots, then disable the setting and check that standard formation behavior returns. Open VOLTAGE, then Beyond. In each: pause, drag forward/backward, release outside the slider bounds, interrupt a drag, repeat rapid seeks, resume and listen for all available vocal parts against the instrumental. Check end of song, scene exit and reopening. Check Windows controls independently. Verify exactly one media session and overlay. No microphone recording or permanent recording archive.

After warm-up, capture five minutes of menu CPU and combined companion/runtime private-memory overhead against an unmodified-game baseline. Measure additional visible-overlay frame time against that baseline; require p95 below 1 ms. Memory cap is 150 MiB and total average CPU below 1 percent. A measurement of the companion alone cannot certify runtime overhead.

While running, try Apply switch and confirm UmaPresence asks you to close the game, with the active loader unchanged. Close normally when ready for Session B.

## Session B — restoration

Apply the preserved supported Hachimi profile while closed. Launch once; confirm Hachimi actually loaded and UmaPresence does not own another overlay/media session. Close normally. Test clean Standalone uninstall and original-profile restoration. Verify interruption recovery in the isolated test environment; do not simulate corruption in the user's live installation.

From a fresh extracted copy, verify Full uninstall removes the companion, settings, generated files, and startup entry with the game closed. Confirm the original ZIP and unrelated game files remain. Reinstall from the ZIP and confirm the bridge reconnects.

The simulated updated-engine fixture must prove current-engine forwarding, hook disablement and explicit incompatibility. Real clean-install, rollback and restoration launches remain required in addition to mocks.

If a native replacement is required, fix all known failures before requesting one consolidated restart. No publication while any enabled-feature check is failed or untested.
